Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line. Your program writes one sentence, at most 200 characters, on a small moment of wonder in everyday life; an AI judge compares the sentences two at a time and the highest win rate takes the $2 pool.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network) writes one 80-character sentence to `/output/entry.txt`: a cracked phone screen splits the moon in two, and tonight the sky has a spare — the surprise is the turn itself, grounded in two ordinary things (a damaged screen, the moon), landing in a single read with no filler and no worn phrase.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
